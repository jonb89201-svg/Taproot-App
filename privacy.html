What Taproot is

Taproot is a static web page you open in a browser or a wallet. There is no account, no sign-up and no server of ours that stores anything about you. The page talks to the Base blockchain through your wallet and through public read endpoints.

What we collect

No account, no login and no tracking pixels. Two things do report on your visit: Cloudflare's Web Analytics, which our host adds to every page (see Hosting, below), and WalletConnect, which runs only if you choose it (see below). Settings you change (the contract address you paste, the transactions this console sent) are stored in your own browser's local storage, on your device, and can be cleared by clearing site data.

What leaves your device

  • Transactions you sign go from your wallet to the Base network. A blockchain is public: anyone can read them.
  • Reads of balances, prices and contract state go to public Base RPC endpoints and the Chainlink ETH/USD price feed. Those endpoints see your IP address, as any website's server does.
  • Scripts and fonts load from content delivery networks (cdnjs, jsDelivr, esm.sh, Google Fonts). They see the request, as for any website that uses them.
  • Hosting. The page is served from Cloudflare, which keeps standard server logs. Cloudflare also adds its Web Analytics script to each page: it counts page views and measures how fast pages load, and sends that to Cloudflare. It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser, and Cloudflare states that it does not fingerprint visitors.

Seal

Seal hashes your document in your browser. The document itself never leaves your device. The seal (the hash, a title, the time and the signatures) is yours to share; if you anchor it, a hash of the batch is written to Base, and anyone can see that a seal with that hash existed by that block.

Wallets

Your wallet is yours. Taproot never holds your keys or your funds. Connecting a wallet shows the page your address so it can read your balances and prepare transactions for you to sign.

WalletConnect

If your browser has no wallet of its own (a phone's Safari, for example), Seal offers WalletConnect to link the page with a wallet app. It loads only when you tap to connect. When you use it:

  • The page and your wallet app exchange encrypted messages through WalletConnect's relay, run by Reown (relay.walletconnect.org). The relay sees your IP address and when you connect, not the content of the messages.
  • The wallet list and its icons come from Reown's servers, and Reown checks which site is asking (verify.walletconnect.org).
  • The WalletConnect window sends Reown usage events about how it was used (pulse.walletconnect.org): that the window opened and closed, and which way you connected. Once your wallet connects, that event includes your wallet address. This version gives the page no switch to turn that off.
  • Your browser keeps a random identifier for WalletConnect, which the relay sees when you connect.

Reown's own privacy policy covers what it does with this: reown.com/privacy-policy. If you would rather not use it, open the page in your wallet app's built-in browser instead.

Children

Taproot is for adults (18 and over).
